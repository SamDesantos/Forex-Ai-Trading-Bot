**Forex Ai Trading Bot** is a high-performance, institutional-grade autonomous desktop trading terminal tailored for quantitative and algorithmic Forex traders. Powered by an ultra-lightweight desktop engine and multi-model AI consensus algorithms, it delivers sub-millisecond execution simulations, live global session tracking, real-time Order Book DOM depth, advanced risk safeguards, and algorithmic trade automation.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. Multi-AI Quantitative Consensus Engine
- **Multi-Model Market Analysis**: Cross-examines technical setups and macro bias across top reasoning models (ChatGPT, Claude, DeepSeek, GLM, Grok, and Gemini).
- **Consensus & Confidence Scoring**: Generates aggregated sentiment gauges, directional conviction scores, entry/invalidation levels, and risk-to-reward ratios before placing trades.
- **Fundamental & Macro Sentiment**: Real-time evaluation of central bank policy biases, economic calendar catalysts, and breaking financial news.

### 2. Live Forex Trading Terminal & Charting
- **Institutional Interactive Charts**: Powered by high-speed candlestick engines with customizable intervals (1m, 5m, 15m, 1h, 4h, 1D).
- **Technical Indicator Suite**: Built-in Bollinger Bands, RSI, MACD, EMAs (20/50/200), ATR volatility metrics, and support/resistance zones.
- **Fast Execution Panel**: Instant Buy/Sell limit, market, and stop orders with one-click TP/SL calculation, custom lot sizes, and configurable leverage up to 1:500.
- **Live Order Book & Time & Sales (DOM Tape)**: Real-time visual bid/ask order book depth with liquidity spread monitoring and transaction flow.

### 3. Global Forex Market Sessions Monitor
- **Active Market Clocks**: Live status tracking for major financial centers:
  - **Sydney** (07:00 – 16:00 AEST)
  - **Tokyo** (09:00 – 18:00 JST)
  - **London** (08:00 – 17:00 GMT)
  - **New York** (08:00 – 17:00 EST)
- **High-Volatility Overlap Alerts**: Automatic indicators during the London/New York session overlap, alerting traders to prime liquidity windows.

### 4. Autonomous Bot & Algorithmic Strategies
- **Automated Execution Engine**: Run multi-pair automated trading loops with strict risk checks.
- **Built-in Pre-Tuned Strategies**:
  - *Institutional Trend Rider* (Multi-timeframe trend following)
  - *Mean Reversion / Volatility Scalper* (Bollinger/RSI extremes)
  - *Breakout & Liquidity Sweep* (Support/Resistance levels)
  - *AI Neural Sentiment Momentum* (Consensus-driven positioning)
- **Strategy Backtester**: Backtest historical data with detailed performance breakdowns: Sharpe ratio, maximum drawdown, profit factor, win rate, and equity curve visualizations.

### 5. Institutional Risk Management & Circuit Breakers
- **Daily Drawdown Limiters**: Automatic bot pausing when daily or total loss thresholds are approached.
- **Emergency Circuit Breaker**: Single-click instant emergency stop button to halt all algorithmic cycles and flatten exposed positions.
- **Dynamic Position Sizing**: Capital preservation rules restricting maximum exposure per currency pair and total margin utilization.
- **Live Margin & Free Margin Analytics**: Real-time margin call warning monitors and leverage exposure gauges.
